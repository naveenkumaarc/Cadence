# Cadence icon

The Cadence mark is a fixed Rose `#E5384F` circle containing four white, round-capped waveform bars. Its brand colour is independent of the in-app accent picker, while the simple waveform remains clear at launcher and notification sizes.

`cadence-icon.svg` is the editable vector master and `cadence-icon-512.png` is the store/README export. Android uses separate Rose background, bars-only foreground and bars-only monochrome vector drawables. The complete glyph stays within the central 66dp of the 108dp adaptive canvas. Density-specific PNGs are generated from the same original vector for legacy launcher handling.

`cadence-icon-previews.svg` and its PNG export show the adaptive artwork under circle, squircle, rounded-square and Android themed-icon treatments on light and dark wallpapers.
